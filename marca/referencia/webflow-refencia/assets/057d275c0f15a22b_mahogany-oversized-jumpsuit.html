Mahogany Oversized Jumpsuit

Series 01
Linear Core (SS26)

A study in exaggerated proportions and thermal insulation. The Olive Sculpted Puffer is engineered to redefine the body’s silhouette through a multi-tiered baffel system. By utilizing a high-density matte nylon shell and reclaimed down fill, the garment achieves a rigid, architectural form that maintains its shape regardless of movement. It is a protective shell designed for the urban void.

Description & Gallery

A Study in Volume.

The 014 Overcoat is an exercise in subtractive pattern-making. By removing the traditional side seams and utilizing a single-piece back panel, the garment creates a continuous, unbroken silhouette that draped independently of the wearer's frame. It is a protective shell designed for the urban void.
